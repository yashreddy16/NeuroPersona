# 🧠 NeuroPersona AI

### AI-Powered Personality Insight and Self-Development Platform

NeuroPersona AI is an intelligent personality assessment and self-development platform that combines the scientifically validated **Big Five Inventory-10 (BFI-10)** personality model with **Google Gemini AI** to generate personalized insights, identify strengths and challenges, and create a customized 30-day growth roadmap.

Unlike traditional personality tests that only describe personality traits, NeuroPersona AI transforms personality assessment into an actionable self-improvement experience by providing tailored recommendations based on each user's unique personality profile.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://neuro-persona.vercel.app/

---

## ✨ Features

* 📋 BFI-10 based personality assessment
* 🧠 Big Five (OCEAN) personality trait analysis
* 🤖 AI-powered personality interpretation using Google Gemini
* 💪 Identification of strengths and improvement areas
* 📅 Personalized 30-Day Self-Development Roadmap
* 📊 Interactive personality visualization dashboard
* ⚡ Real-time assessment and result generation
* 📱 Responsive and user-friendly interface

---

## 🚀 Why NeuroPersona AI?

Most personality assessment platforms stop at telling users **who they are**.

NeuroPersona AI goes a step further by helping users understand:

* Why they behave the way they do
* What their strengths and weaknesses are
* Which areas require improvement
* How they can work on themselves through a structured development plan

By combining psychometric assessment with generative AI, NeuroPersona AI bridges the gap between personality analysis and practical self-development.

## 🔄 Workflow

1. User completes the BFI-10 assessment.
2. Responses are processed using psychometric scoring rules.
3. OCEAN personality scores are calculated.
4. Scores are sent to Gemini AI.
5. AI generates:

   * Personality Insights
   * Strengths
   * Challenges
   * Personalized Recommendations
   * 30-Day Development Roadmap
6. Results are displayed through an interactive dashboard.
